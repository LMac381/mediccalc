NIH Stroke Scale

How to administer
  • Go in order and score the first attempt. Don't coach or repeat.
  • 1b: ask the month and their age. Only exact answers count.
  • 1c: "Open and close your eyes", then "Grip and let go" with the non-weak hand.
  • 2: test horizontal eye movement only.
  • 3: test each quadrant by finger counting or visual threat.
  • 4: "Show me your teeth, raise your eyebrows, squeeze your eyes shut."
  • 5–6: test one limb at a time. Arms out 10 s (90° sitting / 45° lying), palms down; legs at 30° lying for 5 s. Count aloud.
  • 7: finger-nose and heel-shin on both sides. Score only if the ataxia is out of proportion to weakness.
  • 8: pinprick or touch on face, arm, trunk and leg; compare sides.
  • 9–10: without the picture cards, use conversation, naming objects you show, and repeating phrases. Judge clarity of speech for dysarthria.
  • 11: touch both sides at once and show fingers in both visual fields. Ignoring one side is extinction.
